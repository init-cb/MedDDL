How to update deadlines

Edit _data/conferences.yml, add the official source URL, and keep uncertain dates as TBD. This keeps the tracker auditable and avoids misleading submission dates.

Suggested scope

Prioritize major radiology, cardiology, MRI, CMR, neurology, oncology, nuclear medicine, respiratory, and medical AI meetings. Workshop and challenge deadlines are welcome when linked to official sources.

MedDDL is an open-source static tracker. Data may change; verify every deadline on the official conference website.
